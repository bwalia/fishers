Every club runs on the same three questions: who is available, what is the score, and what happened afterwards. Fishers holds all three in one place, so none of them live in a group chat that scrolls away by Tuesday.

AVAILABILITY, ASKED ONCE
Everyone marks themselves available, unavailable or doubtful for a fixture. You pick the side from what comes back, instead of reading a thread twice and counting on your fingers.

FIXTURES
Every match with the ground, the start time and the opposition. One list, in order, for everyone.

LIVE SCORING, BALL BY BALL
Score from the boundary on a phone. Every ball, every over, extras, wickets and how each one fell. Anyone following from home sees it as it happens.

THE SCORECARD, DONE PROPERLY
A full card at the end of the innings: batting, bowling, partnerships and the result, laid out the way a scorebook lays it out.

THE NUMBERS AFTERWARDS
Averages, strike rates, economy and best figures, by player and by club, across the season. The things people argue about in the car park, settled.

YOUR CLUB
Members, teams and the club's own record. Belong to one club or several.

A CHAT THAT STAYS ON THE SUBJECT
Threads per team and per match, so the fixture talk is not buried under everything else.

ENGLISH AND PUNJABI
The whole app, not only the menus — the scoring words too. Switch language whenever you like.

For clubs on a public pitch and clubs with a pavilion. No adverts. No in-app purchases.

Gully Cricket is a club app for cricket. Everything past the first screen belongs to
a club, so review needs the account above — there is nothing meaningful to see
signed out.

The account is a member of "Gully Cricket Demo", a club kept invite-only so it
stays out of the way of real sides.

Signed in with it you will find:

* Home — the next fixture and anything live.
* Fixtures — a match list. Open one and mark yourself available to see how a
  side gets picked.
* A live match — "Score" on a fixture opens ball-by-ball scoring. Tapping runs,
  a wicket or an extra updates the card for everyone watching at once.
* Clubs — the club, its teams and its members.
* Profile — batting and bowling figures, and the language switch. The app is
  fully English and Punjabi, scoring words included.

Reporting and blocking
----------------------

* Press and hold any message in a club chat to report it or block whoever sent
  it.
* A shop listing and a player's profile each carry the same two actions in the
  top-right menu.
* A block works both ways and takes effect at once: neither person sees the
  other's messages or listings again.
* Profile > Blocked people lists everyone you have blocked, and lifts a block.

Reports go to the club's administrators, who can act on them from the web
dashboard at www.gullycricket.app.

Sign in with Apple is offered on the first screen and works with the account
above or with any Apple ID.

There are no in-app purchases and no adverts. The Shop section is classified
listings between club members: people message each other about second-hand
gear, and no payment is taken in the app.

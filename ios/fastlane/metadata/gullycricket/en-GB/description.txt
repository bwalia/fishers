A side does not need a pavilion to need a scorer. Gully Cricket holds what any team holds: who is turning up, what the score is, and what everyone averaged by the end of the season.

WHO IS PLAYING
Everyone marks themselves available, unavailable or doubtful for a match. You pick the side from the answers, not from a hundred messages.

MATCHES
Every match with the ground, the time and the opposition, in one list, for everyone.

LIVE SCORING, BALL BY BALL
Score from the side of the pitch on a phone. Every ball, every over, extras, wickets and how each one fell. Anyone not there follows it live.

THE FULL SCORECARD
Batting, bowling, partnerships and the result at the end of the innings, laid out the way a scorebook does.

THE SEASON'S NUMBERS
Averages, strike rates, economy and best figures, by player and by team. Settled, not remembered differently by everyone.

YOUR TEAM
Members, sides and the team's own record. Play for one or for several.

A CHAT PER MATCH
Threads per side and per match, so the talk about Sunday is not lost under everything else.

ENGLISH AND PUNJABI
All of it, not only the menus — the scoring words too. Change language whenever you like.

Tape ball or leather, matting or turf, a park or a maidan. No adverts. No in-app purchases.
